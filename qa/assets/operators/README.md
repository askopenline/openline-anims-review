# Operator logo assets

> Openline company documentation: maintained in [askopenline/openline-anims-review](https://github.com/askopenline/openline-anims-review). See the [repository README](../../../README.md) for ownership, scope, and release boundaries.

These are presentation assets for the existing [Multi Tier-1 review page](https://openline-anims-review.vercel.app/qa/multiple-tier1). They do not verify partner relationships, network availability or permission to use a mark in production.

## Existing marks

The existing 36-name roster is preserved in `qa/operators.json`, including each original local asset path. Derived WebP files remove excess edge padding and resize proportionally to a maximum 300 × 112 pixels, preserving brand colours and the original files.

## Corrected mappings

Three image/name mismatches in the source grid are corrected only in this showcase:

- **Telefónica:** Its entry incorrectly repeated Movistar. The replacement is the 2021 Telefónica logo from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Telef%C3%B3nica_2021_logo.svg), consistent with the identity described on [Telefónica’s own brand page](https://www.telefonica.com/en/about-us/brands/). Downloaded image: https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/79/Telef%C3%B3nica_2021_logo.svg/1280px-Telef%C3%B3nica_2021_logo.svg.png?utm_source=commons.wikimedia.org&utm_campaign=index&utm_content=thumbnail
- **Three:** Its entry incorrectly repeated Vodafone. Replacement from [Logotyp’s Three UK page](https://logotyp.us/logo/three-uk/), original SVG: https://logotyp.us/file/three-uk.svg
- **SK Telecom:** Its entry displayed KT. Replacement from [Logo.wine’s SK Telecom page](https://www.logo.wine/logo/SK_Telecom), original image: https://download.logo.wine/logo/SK_Telecom/SK_Telecom-Logo.wine.png

The original downloaded replacements are retained alongside the resized WebP derivatives. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners; asset sourcing does not establish an Openline partnership or grant a commercial licence.
